    About the books behind these shorts

    Kotler on Marketing (1999)

    Full title: Kotler on Marketing: How to Create, Win, and Dominate Markets. Free Press. Kotler’s own short version of his thinking for business owners and managers, not students. It covers strategic marketing (finding opportunities, value propositions), the tactical tools, how to plan and control marketing, and how the internet was changing it.

    The textbooks

    Marketing Management (with Kevin Lane Keller, first edition 1967) and Principles of Marketing (with Gary Armstrong). Segmentation, product levels, lifetime value, the buying center and the product life cycle come from here.

    Marketing 4.0 (2016)

    Written with Hermawan Kartajaya and Iwan Setiawan of MarkPlus, Indonesia. It explains how connected customers buy, using the 5A path. Useful if your customers are Shopee and Tokopedia sellers.
